A task guide starts from the work, not the tool. Each one has two layers. **Do the work:** a numbered set of steps you can begin immediately, with a starter instruction to copy. **Govern the work:** the checks that make the result safe to use and possible to resume later: what you decide, what to check, what to keep, and when to stop and ask. Each guide ends with the boundaries to confirm before the assistant begins.

## How task guides grow

Guided first sessions are designed to leave a task guide behind, or to improve one. If you have a task that should be a guide, [tell your program guide](~/support/index.html). Contributed guides are reviewed against the program's rules for evidence, language, data, and authority before they are published, and they credit the people who wrote them.
