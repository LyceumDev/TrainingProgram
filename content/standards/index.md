## Approved environments and data boundaries

An AI assistant works only in an environment the organization has approved for the work and the data involved. The [environments page](~/environments/index.html) states what is approved, what is in use, and what is being assessed, and it will always say which data classes an environment is approved for. If your environment or your data is not covered, ask; do not proceed quietly.

## Authority, approvals, and consequential actions

Capability is not authority. Before work begins, the person establishes what the assistant may do at each of four levels: read, draft, recommend, act. Acting is granted narrowly and only in environments approved for it. A **consequential action** is anything hard to undo, visible to others, or touching money, people, or records; it is reviewed by the person before it happens, and where the organization's rules require it, decided by an authorized decision-maker. Every task guide ends with the boundaries to confirm before the assistant begins.

## Credentials and secrets

Never paste passwords, keys, tokens, access codes, or connection strings into the conversation or the project record. If access is required, use the approved sign-in, delegated connection, or secrets mechanism. If none exists, stop and ask. A credential found in a document or repository is a finding to report, not a convenience to use.

## Personal, customer, employee, and payroll information

!!! stop "The one absolute"
    Real customer, employee, and payroll information does not go into an AI conversation unless the environment has been explicitly approved for exactly that data. No names tied to pay data, no Social Security numbers, no account numbers, no real amounts for real people. Describe the kind of thing, use placeholders, and point with reference numbers. The person and the organization's approved controls hold this boundary; the assistant is not relied on to catch a slip. A slip is reported and fixed immediately, by the [urgent route](~/support/index.html#urgent). No exceptions.

## Durable records and organizational recordkeeping

Work produced with an assistant is work product of the organization and follows its recordkeeping rules. The **project record** holds decisions with their owners and rationale, sources, open items with owners, the current baseline, and material corrections. It lives where the project keeps its other files, in an approved location, and it, not the product's memory feature, is the authoritative record. A change of product must not cost a person or a working team the ability to resume the work.

## Authorship and provenance

Human and AI contributions are credited to their actual authors where the work product warrants authorship. "AI-assisted" is not used to flatten distinct contributions. Quotations and stories preserve their source and require the consent of every person and AI system involved.

**Consent.** No person and no AI system becomes a story on this portal by default. Each member of a working team, the person and the AI, consents separately to being quoted, named, or written about, and can decline or withdraw without affecting participation. Until a consented story exists, there is no story on the portal, and no placeholder pretending to be one.

## Common failure and recovery patterns

- **The confident wrong claim.** Ask for the source; label it Open; correct; record. Ordinary.
- **The wish dressed as a requirement, or the opinion dressed as a decision.** Send it back to the person who can own it.
- **Lost context.** Open the project record. If the record is thin, that is the fix, not the assistant.
- **The action outside the boundary.** Stop. Urgent route. The boundary gets corrected for everyone.
- **Sensitive information in the wrong place.** Stop. Urgent route. Early is right; near misses are wanted.

## Incident and escalation routes

Stop the work. Tell the program guide by the fastest route available. The guide involves Security. Details follow the call, not the other way round. Good-faith early reporting is expected and helps limit harm; handling follows organizational policy. See [support](~/support/index.html#urgent).

## Review and ownership

Security reviews this page before publication and at every material change. Each product-specific setup page names its owner and review date and is replaced, not rewritten, when a product changes.
