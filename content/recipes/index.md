A recipe starts from the work, not the tool. Each one answers the same seven questions: what you are trying to accomplish, what the assistant needs from you, what it can carry, what you decide, what to check, what to keep, and when to stop and ask. Each states its authority plainly, so there are far fewer surprises in what the assistant does.

## How recipes grow

Guided first sessions are designed to leave a recipe behind, or to improve one. If you have a task that should be a recipe, [tell your program guide](~/support/index.html). Contributed recipes are reviewed against the program's rules for evidence, language, data, and authority before they are published, and they credit the people who wrote them.
