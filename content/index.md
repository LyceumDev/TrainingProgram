## What an AI assistant is

An **AI assistant** is software that can help research, analyze, draft, and organize work, and, when connected to approved tools, carry out narrowly authorized actions. You provide the purpose, the context, the judgment, and the approval. You verify material outputs and remain accountable for work used by the organization.

Used this way, the assistant is not a one-off prompt box. It is a continuing collaborator on your project: it works from the context you give it, takes on the heavy parts of the work, and keeps a record with you so the work can resume tomorrow. It is not an oracle, and it does not make the organization's decisions. The rule that holds all of it together:

> **You establish the assistant's authority, review consequential actions, and remain accountable for what enters the organization's work.**

An assistant's ability to do something is not permission to do it. Capability is not authority.

## Where will the assistant work?

Setup depends on the approved place the assistant will work, not on whether you think of yourself as technical. The choices are [an approved browser or business application](~/environments/index.html#browser), [a repository and development workspace](~/environments/index.html#repository), [a specialized operational environment](~/environments/index.html#operational), or the answer most people give first: **[I don't know yet, help me choose](~/support/i-dont-know-yet.html)**. That answer is complete, and it routes to a person.

## The recommended start is a guided first session

Bring one real task you are permitted to work on. A program guide sits with you and the assistant for about an hour. The aim is that you leave with a useful, verified piece of work and a record you can resume from, and that the portal gets better because of what you found hard. [Book one here.](~/support/request-a-guided-session.html)
