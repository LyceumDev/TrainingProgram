## Good. That is the most common answer.

Nobody is expected to recognize "repository workspace" or "operational environment" before the program has taught them what those are. If you do not know where the assistant should work, you have not failed a test. You have given the answer that routes to a person.

## What happens

You [request a guided first session](~/support/request-a-guided-session.html) and leave the environment choice at "I don't know yet." The guide asks you three questions, usually in a short reply:

1. **What work will you bring?** Writing, analysis, research, support, development, something else.
2. **Which systems does it touch?** Documents and email; a code repository; an operational system; none of these.
3. **What kind of information is in it?** General business material; anything about real customers, employees, or pay.

From those three answers the guide chooses the environment, tells you which setup page applies once it is approved, and, if your environment is still being assessed, runs your first session in the guide's approved environment with non-sensitive material, so that the session still aims to leave you with something useful.

## What you do not have to do

Read the lessons first. Install anything. Know any of the words on the [environments page](~/environments/index.html). Be technical.
