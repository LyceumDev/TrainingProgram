## Talk to a person

Your **program guide** is Osiris Maldonado. Message or email at any time: stuck, curious, something feels off, a question that seems too small, an assistant that raised something it could not resolve. Every one of those is exactly what the guide is for, and feedback in both directions is how the program improves.

## Book a guided first session

The recommended start for everyone. One real, permitted task; a guide beside you and the assistant for about an hour; the aim is a useful, verified piece of work and a record you can resume from. [Request one.](~/support/request-a-guided-session.html)

## Urgent: data, security, or an action concern {: #urgent }

!!! stop "Use this route right away if"
    - real customer, employee, or payroll information went into an AI conversation that was not approved for it;
    - an assistant took, or is about to take, an action you did not authorize or are not sure was allowed;
    - a credential, key, or password may have been exposed;
    - anything else feels like a security or data concern.

**What to do:** stop the work. Tell your program guide immediately, by the fastest route you have. The guide involves Security. Include what happened, when, in which environment, and what information or action was involved; the details can follow the first call.

Good-faith early reporting is expected and helps limit harm; handling follows organizational policy. Near misses are wanted as much as incidents; they are how the boundaries get better.

## Something on this portal does not work for you

A link, a page that does not work with your screen reader, a task guide that assumes something you do not have. Tell the guide. We treat accessibility defects as priority fixes.
