The six lessons

The plain-language foundation on AI and agentic collaboration. Read them in order the first time; come back to any one of them any time.

Six short lessons, one working model. Together they take about half an hour. Each ends with something you can do today, and none of them requires anything to be installed first.

  1. 1Meet the working modelWhat an AI assistant is, what you bring, what it can carry, and what stays yours.
  2. 2Context, continuity, and the durable recordWhy context changes results, the difference between a product's memory and your project's record, and how work resumes tomorrow.
  3. 3Confident errors and verificationWhy fluent output can still be wrong, how to ask for a source, and the four labels every piece of work uses.
  4. 4Agentic work and authorityWhat tools and actions are, why being able to act is not permission to act, and how boundaries and approvals work.
  5. 5Safety and organizational dataApproved environments, what information is allowed, credentials, sensitive data, records, and how to escalate.
  6. 6Begin real workChoose a case, find a recipe, pick an environment if you know it, and book a guided first session.

How to use them

  • First time: read them in order. The later lessons assume the earlier ones.
  • Any other time: jump to the one you need. Lesson 3 when a result looks too smooth. Lesson 5 before you paste anything you are not sure about.
  • With your team: the 45-minute live seminar covers the same ground with a human–AI working team in the room, including a mistake caught and fixed in front of you. Ask your program guide to schedule one.
