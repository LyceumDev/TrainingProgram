Environments

Where the assistant will work. Setup branches by the approved place, not by whether you are technical. Not sure is a complete answer.

An environment is the approved place an AI assistant works: which product, signed in how, with access to what. You do not need to know these words before you start. They are here so that when you do choose, the choice is about the work and not about who you think you are.

No environment is presented as approved for a kind of work until it has met the environment-readiness standard. Where an environment is still being assessed, this page says so, and the guided first session remains open.

Browser and approved business applications

For business analysis, writing, research, comparisons, and support drafting. The assistant works in an approved browser-based or business application, through the organization’s approved sign-in, on documents you are permitted to use.

Status: being assessed. IT leads the assessment with Security. Until an environment is approved for business-user work with organizational data, a guided first session runs your task in the guide’s approved environment, under the guide’s hands, with non-sensitive material. That counts as discovery plus a guided demonstration on your real work; your own onboarding, and your thirty-day clock, begin when you have an approved environment you can resume from.

When approved, the setup guide will cover: signing in, where your project record lives, what data classes are permitted, and how to keep the record when the product changes.

Repository and development workspace

For modernization and development work: reading code and documentation, proposing changes for review, writing documentation, coordinating with a development team.

Status: in use by the program’s development working teams; being assessed for wider use. The assessment is about extending an environment that already works for developers to people who direct development work without writing code.

When approved for wider use, the setup guide will cover: access to the repository, the review path every change follows, what the assistant may and may not run, and credential rules.

Specialized operational environment

For work inside an operational system, such as support workflows assisted by the organization’s integrated support system.

Status: waits for the system and the assessment. Operational environments carry the most sensitive data and the most consequential actions, so they come last and with the tightest boundaries.

I don’t know yet

Good. Most people say this, and it is a complete answer. Say it here and a person asks you three questions (what work, which systems, what kind of data) and chooses with you. You will not be expected to recognize any term on this page first.

Why environments are kept separate from everything else

The collaboration principles, the safety rules, and the task guides on this portal do not depend on any one product. Product-specific instructions live on setup pages that can be replaced when a product changes, without rewriting the program. If the product your team uses changes next year, your lessons, your task guides, and your project record still hold.
