Customer support

Intake, triage, response, escalation, and follow-up, and, when it is ready, workflows assisted by the organization's integrated support system.

What working with an assistant looks like in this work

Support work is a stream of small decisions under time pressure: what is this really about, how urgent is it, who should see it, what do we say, and what has to happen next. An AI assistant can carry the reading and the drafting in that stream: pulling the facts out of a long message, proposing a triage, drafting a response in the house tone, and keeping the thread’s record straight. You keep the decisions, because you know the customer, the history, and what the organization can promise.

The one thing this case has more of than any other is sensitive information. Real people, real accounts, real amounts. That is why the boundary is stated first here.

The boundary, first

In support work the one absolute is daily, not occasional

Real customer, employee, and payroll information does not go into an AI conversation unless the environment has been explicitly approved for exactly that data. Describe the kind of issue. Use the case number. Say “the customer” or “Employee A.” The assistant can triage, draft, and structure without ever seeing the name attached to the account.

What you can do now

  • Read the six lessons, especially lesson 5.
  • Practice with non-sensitive material: a made-up ticket, or a real one with every identifying detail replaced. The problem statement and memo check task guides work well on support messages and draft replies.
  • Book a guided first session and bring the kind of ticket you handle most. The guide will show you where an assistant helps and where the line is.

What waits

The complete case, with task guides for intake, triage, response drafting, escalation, and follow-up, is published when an approved environment exists for support work and when the organization’s integrated support system (OTIS) is ready for assistant-supported workflows. Until then, this page is an honest preview, not a promise.
